CHESS - where the borrowed parts came from
==========================================

The game itself was written for this site. Two things in it were made by
other people, who gave them away for anyone to use.

THE SOUNDS  (sfx/)
------------------
Every recording is from freesound.org and was released by the person who
uploaded it into the public domain (Creative Commons Zero,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each sound's own page
was checked for that licence on 2026-11-20 before the file was fetched.
Credit is not required by CC0; it is given anyway, with thanks.

What was done to them: the files are freesound's preview copies. The .mp3
files are exactly as they came. The .wav files were cut down to the part
that was wanted, faded at both ends so they do not click, levelled so that
no button is far louder than another, and saved as 22 kHz mono. The moves
and captures are single knocks cut out of one longer recording of chess
pieces being put down.

sfx.json is the list the game reads: "game" says which clips play at which
moment, "board" is the soundboard in button order. Moments with no
recording (the clock ticking, a pawn promoting, a menu click) are made by
the game itself as plain tones.
