Every sound in sfx.wav is a recording released into the public domain
(Creative Commons Zero, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They were trimmed, faded at both ends, levelled and laid end to end;
sfx.json says where each one starts and how long it is.
